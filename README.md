# 📊 Customer Behavior & Retail Analytics

## 🚀 Project Overview

Customer Behavior & Retail Analytics is an end-to-end Data Analytics project that analyzes retail transaction data to uncover customer purchasing patterns, spending behavior, product preferences, and revenue-driving factors.

The project leverages **Python** for data preprocessing, **MySQL** for business analysis, and **Power BI** for interactive dashboard development. The goal is to help retail organizations make data-driven decisions that improve customer retention, optimize marketing strategies, and increase overall revenue.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Retail companies collect large amounts of transactional data, but often struggle to extract actionable insights from it. Without proper analysis, opportunities for customer engagement, subscription growth, and revenue optimization remain hidden.

### Business Question

**How can customer shopping data be leveraged to improve customer engagement, optimize marketing strategies, increase subscription adoption, and drive higher revenue?**

---

## 📌 Objectives

* Identify customer segments based on purchasing behavior.
* Analyze the impact of discounts on customer spending.
* Evaluate the effectiveness of subscription programs.
* Understand product preferences and customer satisfaction.
* Measure the influence of shipping methods on purchase value.
* Generate actionable business recommendations using data-driven insights.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Customer Spending Trends

Customer spending behavior varies across demographic groups, creating opportunities for targeted marketing campaigns.

### Subscription Impact

Customers enrolled in subscription programs contribute a significant portion of overall revenue.

### Shipping Preferences

Customers choosing premium shipping options generally exhibit higher transaction values.

### Customer Loyalty

A relatively small group of loyal customers contributes a substantial share of total revenue.

### Product Performance

Certain products consistently achieve stronger customer ratings and purchase frequency.

### Promotion Effectiveness

Discount programs successfully encourage purchases but require careful profitability monitoring.
